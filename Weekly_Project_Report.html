Section 2
Problems & Challenges
  • Hindi and English text are present in the certificates, making text-based OCR verification more challenging.
  • Scanned documents may contain rotation, translation, scaling, uneven lighting, blur, compression artifacts, and scanner noise.
  • Direct pixel-by-pixel comparison can produce false differences unless the scanned document is first aligned and normalized.
  • The certificate verification process has a zero-tolerance requirement, so automated detection should assist a human reviewer rather than be treated as the final authority.
  • Further investigation is required to distinguish genuine printing/layout errors from differences caused by scanning conditions.
  • Frontend prototype uses mock data; real verification requires backend implementation.
  • Ensuring seamless integration between the React.js frontend and FastAPI backend when backend is ready.
